Parking SMS helps you pay for SMS parking, runs an active timer, and alerts you with a full-screen alarm so you never forget to stop parking.

Features:
• Sends start and stop SMS with customizable message templates
• Remembers cities, parking zones, and license plate numbers
• Automatically formats and cleans plate and zone inputs
• Three timer modes: Alarm only, Auto-stop at end, or Alarm then auto-stop after a grace period
• Full-screen alarm over the lock screen with snooze and stop controls
• Ongoing notification with a quick stop button
• Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile
• Parking session history with CSV export
• Safe test mode to simulate sending messages
• Fully offline: zero internet permission, no ads, no trackers, no Google Play Services
