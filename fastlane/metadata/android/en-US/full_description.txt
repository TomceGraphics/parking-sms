In many towns you start parking by texting "<zone> <plate>" to a city number and stop it by texting "S". It never stops by itself, so it is easy to forget and overpay. Parking SMS sends those messages for you, runs a timer, and rings a full-screen alarm until you act.

<b>Highlights</b>
• One-tap Pay with your city, zone, plate and timer on a single screen
• Zones are remembered automatically, plates are cleaned as you type (bt-1110 ab becomes BT1110AB)
• Timer modes: alarm only, auto-stop at the end, or alarm then auto-stop after a grace period
• Optional reminder before the end, snooze, and a full-screen alarm over the lock screen
• Ongoing notification with a Stop button, survives reboots
• Retries a failed SMS once and never fails silently
• Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile
• History with CSV export, editable SMS templates, Test mode that never sends anything
• Light and dark themes, optional Material You colors

<b>Private by design</b>
No internet permission, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no Google services. Everything stays on your phone. Free software under GPL-3.0.

<b>Good to know</b>
Standard SMS rates from your carrier apply. The app is not affiliated with any city or parking operator. For reliable alarms, allow exact alarms and full-screen alerts and set battery usage to Unrestricted.
